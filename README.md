# The toddlers have bazookas now: sources and data

Sources and data for the talk **“The toddlers have bazookas now”** by Jeroen Vereecken.

**Page:** https://jeroen-vereecken.github.io/Devoxx2026/

- [`index.html`](index.html): every source quoted or counted on the slides, in slide order.
- [`data/talk-ratings.csv`](data/talk-ratings.csv): 168 Devoxx Belgium 2026 talks, each rated by `claude-sonnet-5-5` from its transcript on five ways of working with AI (guardrails, human in the loop, spec-driven, AI reviews AI, full speed ahead), with a quote and timestamp per talk.
- [`data/prompt.md`](data/prompt.md): the exact prompt, the fields, and how the slide’s numbers follow from the CSV.
